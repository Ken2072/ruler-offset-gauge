Accurate Circular Saw Cuts Made Easy

Lining up a straight edge or clamping guide for a circular saw usually requires measuring the shoe-to-blade distance every single cut. This customizable bracket locks onto a standard steel ruler, physically setting the exact blade offset without continuous recalculation or test cuts.

1. Measure Your Saw

Measure the exact distance from the straight edge of your saw base plate to the outer tooth of the blade.

2. Set Ruler Tolerances

Measure your ruler width and thickness with calipers. Add at least 0.012" for a smooth sliding fit.

AI-Assisted CAD with a Custom 2D DSL

Instead of calculating complex polygon coordinate lists or CSG intersections by hand, this project uses an AI-friendly coordinate sketch pipeline (plot2d). You can dictate paths using relative moves (u, d, l, r), declare fillet radii (f), and interpolate loops smoothly before linear extrusion.
