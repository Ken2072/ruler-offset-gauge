3D Offset Ruler Gauge for Circular Saws

Custom 3D-printable alignment bracket for repeatable, accurate straightedge cuts

Accurate Circular Saw Cuts Made Easy

Lining up a straightedge clamp guide for a circular saw usually requires measuring and recalculating the shoe-to-blade distance for every single cut. This customizable bracket locks onto your standard steel ruler, physically setting the exact blade offset without continuous re-measuring or test cuts.

1. Measure Your Saw

Measure the exact distance from the straight edge of your saw's base plate (shoe) to the outer tooth of the blade.

2. Set Ruler Tolerances

Measure your ruler width and thickness with calipers. Be sure to include your desired sliding clearance (e.g., at least 0.012").

How to Customize & Export: In the 3D viewer below, adjust the dimension inputs on the panel to match your saw and ruler. Click Export (STL / 3MF) to download a file ready for 3D printing.
