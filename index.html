3D Offset Ruler Gauge for Circular Saws

Parametric 3D-printable bracket for repeatable, dead-accurate straightedge cuts

⚠️ Sizing Notice: Measure your ruler with calipers and add at least 0.012" clearance to Ruler H and Ruler W.

Enter your custom dimensions in the viewer below, inspect the orientation, and export an STL or 3MF.

Required Hardware

  • 1/4" Hex Nut (press-fits into the gauge nut pocket).
  • 1/4" Thumb Screw / Wing Bolt (threads through the nut to lock the gauge securely onto your ruler).
  • Standard Steel Ruler (e.g., 1.43" wide straightedge ruler).

Calibration & Verification Workflow

  1. Test Print with Slicer Pause: Before committing to a full print, slice the bracket and insert a pause command at layer height ~10 mm. Slide your actual steel ruler into the channel while paused to verify smooth sliding clearance before finishing the job.
  2. Refine Clearance if Needed: If the fit is too tight or loose, adjust Ruler H / Ruler W in the customizer and re-export.
  3. Cut Two 5" Test Boards: Set your gauge to 5.00", lock it with the thumbscrew, butt your speed square or straightedge against the fence, and make two clean test cuts.
  4. Verify Accuracy with Calipers: Measure the cut blocks with digital calipers. As shown below, an accurate offset dialed into the gauge consistently yields dead-accurate cuts (e.g., 4.99" / 5.00").
  5. Dial-In Saw Offset: If your cut block measures slightly over or under your target, adjust your Offset parameter by that exact difference and reprint for lifetime accuracy on that circular saw.

Offline / Local Customizer Option

You do not need to use GitHub Pages to customize this model. You can run the script entirely offline in your own browser:

  1. Download the standalone script directly:
    Download gauge_standalone.js
  2. Open https://jscad.app in Chrome, Edge, or Firefox.
  3. Drag and drop gauge_standalone.js from your file explorer directly onto the JSCAD browser window.
  4. Adjust your parameters in the left panel and click Export to generate your STL/3MF locally.
